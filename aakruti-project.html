Aakruti - Clothing Store

Where contemporary design meets cultural craftsmanship in a retail experience

Category
COMMERCIAL
Year
2025
Location
AMB Mall, Hyderabad
Size
3,650 sq. ft.

The Space

In a bustling mall setting, Aakruti's design stands out with a dramatic 'Aa' installation and a sleek, curved wall that commands attention. A strategically placed video wall amplifies visibility, using dynamic digital content to engage passersby and attract walk-in customers through visual storytelling.

The store delivers a cohesive and immersive spatial experience, guided by gracefully curved SS pipe racks and dynamic triangular lighting that animates the space. The sculptural 'Aa' entrance installation serves as a bold brand statement, while a soft, refined color palette fosters a sense of calm.

The Journey

Every detail has a story. Here's how it all came to life.

The Design

Stainless steel rack borders, embellished with sculpted motifs, offer a sturdy yet culturally evocative structure for showcasing garments. Positioned in front, display counters made from luxurious teak wood feature delicate silver inlays, echoing the elegance of traditional Indian silverware influenced by Bidri craftsmanship.

The modern wear section at Aakruti redefines retail display with fluid stainless steel pipes, shaping movement and zones without rigid enclosures. These sculptural forms extend seamlessly to display counters, ensuring a cohesive visual flow.

The Result

Thoughtfully planned zoning enhances comfort, fluid movement, and engaging visual connections throughout the store. The zoned layout ensures smooth navigation and ample display space, with gondolas for added hanging and a dedicated service area offering easy, private access to trial rooms.

Aakruti's rack display transforms the shopping experience with a rhythmic blend of stocked shelves and hanging sections, intuitively guiding customers through the space. Sculpted stainless steel pipes define these zones, providing both structure and seamless visual flow. This dynamic layout enhances product visibility while fostering an engaging and fluid retail journey.

This project masterfully intertwines narrative and design, with SS pipes shaped like flowing threads to form display racks that seamlessly blend function and form. These precisely bent pipes double as partitions, hanging systems, and wayfinding elements, guiding visitors through the space with fluid elegance.

Anchoring the brand identity, the sculptural 'Aa' installation serves as a striking focal point, while triangular stretch lights introduce a sense of dynamism. A muted, refined color palette balances the energy, creating a harmonious environment that fuses contemporary structure with timeless cultural nuances for a truly distinctive spatial experience.
